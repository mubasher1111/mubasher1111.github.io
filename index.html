Blaze Garments: one store, every way to sell

A clothing manufacturer in Lahore sells retail to shoppers, wholesale to boutiques, private label to brands, and custom printed bulk orders. I designed and built the whole site on WooCommerce with a custom theme and custom plugins, then added tracking, SEO and an Excel reporting tool on top.

Most garment sites pick one audience. This one serves four from one catalogue, with a separate path for each: a normal cart for retail, MOQ-based wholesale with an inquiry bag, a private-label service, and a print-on-demand studio that ends in a factory-ready tech pack.

Print-on-demand studio

A buyer picks a blank (12 garments for men, women and kids), a quantity tier, fabric, colour, sizes and label, then places artwork anywhere on the real garment photo, sleeves included. The studio measures print size in inches, warns when artwork leaves the print area, and builds a tech pack PDF with mockups, flats, placements and a measurement chart. The inquiry and all files go straight to the factory team.
